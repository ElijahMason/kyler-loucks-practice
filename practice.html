Sheet 04, schedule

Work you can hire.

Schedule

The schedule is the practice. Each line is a kind of work. The standards under it are the conditions he holds while doing that work.

  1. A

    Cloud architecture

    He draws how accounts, networks, delivery pipelines, and runtime fit, and he writes the constraints on that drawing.

  2. K

    Kubernetes platforms

    He designs the platform contract: how a workload lands, which boundaries it crosses, and what the cluster is for.

  3. T

    Terraform

    Infrastructure is code you can read, review, and apply through the pipeline.

  4. P

    Delivery pipelines

    CI/CD you can explain. GitOps when production should follow the repository.

  5. S

    Serverless where it fits

    He specifies serverless when the work is an event and the operational surface should stay small.

  6. M

    Multi-account structure

    One account strategy, named environments, and boundaries that identity and cost can share.

  7. R

    Cost and reliability review

    You hire the review. The work is to make the bill readable and to name how the system fails. The practice does not publish a claimed result in place of that review.

Standards he holds

  1. One account strategy.
  2. Named environments.
  3. Infrastructure as code.
  4. A pipeline you can explain.
  5. A bill you can read.

Security is a design constraint on every sheet.
