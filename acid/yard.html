Yard limits

Work you can put on the board.

The yard is the practice. You hire a line of work. The limits on the right stay in force while that work is underway.

  • A

    Cloud architecture

    He draws the fit of accounts, networks, delivery, and runtime, and he writes the constraints on that drawing.

  • K

    Kubernetes platforms

    He sets the platform contract: where a workload lands, which block it occupies, and what the cluster is for.

  • T

    Terraform

    The platform is code you can read on the plan block and apply on the lined route.

  • P

    Delivery pipelines

    CI/CD you can walk. GitOps when the repository is the record the route follows.

  • S

    Serverless where it fits

    He lines serverless when the work is an event and the surface to watch should stay small.

  • M

    Multi-account structure

    One account strategy. Named environments. Boundaries that identity and the bill can share.

  • R

    Cost and reliability review

    You hire the review. He reads the bill and names the failure modes. The yard does not post a claimed saving in place of that work.
