Aspect book

Three lamps, and the rule for each.

Every block on the board carries an aspect. The lamp is a mark. The word next to it is the instruction. Fill and label travel together, so the meaning is not carried by color alone.

  1. Stop

    Do not apply. The plan is missing, the environment has no name, the account boundary conflicts with another route, or the bill cannot be read. Security is a design constraint at this lamp: identity, reach, and the data boundary have to be on the route before the aspect changes.

  2. Approach

    The plan is visible and review is open. You may move toward the next block with the constraint still in view. Approach is not a permission to skip the hold.

  3. Clear

    The route is lined, the block ahead is empty, and apply may proceed on the pipeline. Clear is a condition of this block. It is not a report about a client outcome.

Kubernetes takes clear when the workload needs a platform contract. Serverless takes clear when the work is an event and the operational surface should stay small. The aspect follows the workload.
