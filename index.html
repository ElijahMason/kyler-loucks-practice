Practice line

Kyler Loucks is a systems architect and cloud engineer. This site is his contracting practice.

He designs how accounts, networks, delivery pipelines, and runtime fit. The drawing names AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, GitOps, and serverless where the workload fits, along with the multi-account structure those pieces share.

He has publicly shared an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate achievement (2025). On Medium he has published notes on the same craft, including how to calculate IPv4 CIDR blocks for VPCs and subnets.
