051Engagement

Context

An engagement goes better when the first page names the system and the constraints already known. The practice sells the drawing and the work that belongs on it: cloud architecture, Kubernetes platforms, Terraform, delivery pipelines, serverless where it fits, multi-account structure, and a review of cost and reliability.

Decision

Begin with accounts, network, delivery, and runtime on one record. Keep the standards of 014, 027, and 038 in force: one account strategy, named environments, infrastructure as code, a pipeline you can explain, and a bill you can read. Security remains a design constraint. You can hire the cost and reliability review. This page does not substitute a claimed result for that review.

Consequences
