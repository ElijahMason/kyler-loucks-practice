014Account boundary

Context

A request crosses an account boundary. The boundary exists whether or not a drawing shows it. Environments without names collect resources that the next person cannot point to. Networks inherit the same trouble when address ranges collide, which is why an address plan, CIDR included, belongs with the account plan.

Decision

Hold one account strategy. Name every environment. Draw the boundary before a service is added. Multi-account structure is this decision carried out, with identity and the bill able to share the same lines.

Consequences

  • The opening work is an account plan.
  • A resource without an environment name waits.
  • Security is a design constraint on the boundary: who may cross it, and what may move with them.
  • Cost has a place to attach, so a later review can read the bill.
