Loucks 038 Runtime

Accepted as practice

038Runtime

Context

The account plan and the delivery record still need a place for the work to run. Kubernetes and serverless answer different shapes of work. Choosing one because it is familiar leaves the operational surface unmatched to the workload.

Decision

Use Kubernetes when workloads need a shared platform contract: how they land, how they are reached, and what they are allowed to touch. Use serverless where the work is an event and the surface to operate should stay small. Record the reason beside the choice. AWS is the ground these runtimes sit on in this practice.

Consequences

  • The runtime is a decision with a reason.
  • The watch surface stays matched to the work.
  • A platform contract is drawn before a cluster is treated as the default room.
  • An event-shaped workload can stay small without being forced into that cluster.
