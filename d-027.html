027Delivery

Context

A change can leave a working directory and reach production with no record the next operator can walk. The platform then depends on memory. Decision 014 gave the accounts their names. This decision gives the change a path.

Decision

Infrastructure is code. Delivery is a pipeline you can explain on one page. CI/CD is that pipeline. GitOps is the form it takes when production should follow the repository. Terraform is the usual language of the code, because the plan and the apply need a shared text.

Consequences

  • A reviewer starts in the repository.
  • Apply follows the record.
  • A path that cannot be explained does not become the practice.
  • The pipeline is something you can hire him to draw, and then to make legible to the people who run it.
